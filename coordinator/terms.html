Terms of use

Radiology Coordinator Output is intended for one operator’s personal learning workflow. These pages do not offer public accounts, a subscription service or access to the operator’s files.

Intended use

The application organizes workflow outputs and reports. It is not intended to provide clinical decisions, diagnose patients or replace professional judgment. Generated educational content and summaries can contain errors and should be checked against their sources when relied upon.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for authorizing the Google connection, selecting appropriate input and output data, respecting source-material rights, managing retained files, and reviewing changes to the workflow before enabling them. This application does not grant permission to redistribute third-party teaching material or patient information.

Availability and changes

This private workflow may be changed, paused or retired by its operator. There is no stated service-level commitment. Reports and receipts are operational records, not a guarantee that every downstream action completed successfully.

Stopping use

The operator may stop the workflow and revoke Google authorization. Previously created files and reports remain until they are separately removed. See the privacy notice for storage and removal boundaries.
